A Comparative Study: From-Scratch BiLSTM vs Frozen Linear Probe vs XLM-R vs PrahokBART Fine-Tuning

Core Research Question & Verdict: When retrieving official statutory articles for conversational citizen queries, does multilingual transfer learning outperform from-scratch and monolingual models?

1. Problem Formulation & The Limon Font Challenge
⏱️ 0:25 - 0:55 (30s) Background & Engineering

The Citizen Accessibility Bottleneck

  • Domain Problem: Cambodian citizens struggle to locate relevant statutory protections because laws are lengthy, continuous, and highly technical.
  • Task Definition: Given an informal Khmer question, retrieve the exact statutory article (\(k \le 5\)) across the full 1,976-article corpus without statute filtering.
  • Worked Query Example:

    Query: "តើកិច្ចសន្យាបង្កើតឡើងដោយរបៀបណា?" (How is a contract formed?)

    Target: Civil Code Art. 315 ("ការបង្កើតកិច្ចសន្យា")

  • Why Lexical Search Fails: BM25 depends on exact term matches. On informal queries, vocabulary mismatch causes 57.5% of BM25 retrieval failures.

The Limon Legacy Font Challenge

Official PDFs from Open Development Cambodia (ODC) use legacy 8-bit Limon fonts (`Limon S1`, `Limon R1`):

  • Standard PDF tools extract meaningless Latin ASCII: maRta 336>- → visual មាត្រា ៣៣៦.-
  • Finite State Machine (FSM) Syllable Reordering: $$\text{Consonant} + \text{Subscript (ជើង)} + \text{Pre-Vowel} \to \text{Consonant} + \text{Subscript} + \text{Vowel}$$ Reorders left-placed visual vowels (េ, ែ, ៃ) into canonical Unicode.
  • Guaranteed Integrity: 100% consecutive extraction of all 1,976 articles (Civil: 1–1,304, Criminal: 1–672) with complete structural hierarchy.

2. Legal Corpus & Strict Zero-Leakage Protocol
⏱️ 0:55 - 1:25 (30s) Methodology & Integrity

Dual Evaluation Benchmarks

  • Total Corpus: 1,976 statutory articles (Civil Code: 1,304, Criminal Code: 672).
  • Primary Benchmark (T-Q: 200 Questions):
    • 100 Civil Code + 100 Criminal Code questions.
    • Independently authored by Cambodian legal researchers to capture realistic citizen legal dilemmas.
    • Full corpus ranking: 1 query vs 1,976 candidate articles.
  • Secondary Benchmark (T-T: 148 Titles):
    • 148 held-out article titles retrieving body passages.
    • Tests in-distribution statutory title-matching performance.

Four Strict Leakage Guards (Course Mandate)

  • 1. Question Exclusion: All 200 benchmark questions and their 261 citing articles are strictly excluded from the training split.
  • 2. Title Stripping: Statutory titles are stripped from training passages, preventing models from memorizing trivial lexical headings.
  • 3. Fixed Splits: 1,176 training pairs, 130 validation pairs (seed 42). Models selected on validation MRR@10 only.
  • 4. Single Test Evaluation: Test set evaluated strictly once to prevent hyperparameter overfitting on test metrics.

4. Contrastive Objective (InfoNCE) & Learning Dynamics
⏱️ 2:05 - 2:35 (30s) Contrastive Optimization

InfoNCE with In-Batch Negatives

  • For mini-batch of \(B\) pairs \((q_i, p_i)\), all non-matching passages \(p_j\) (\(j \neq i\)) serve as negatives: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = - \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \log \frac{\exp(\text{sim}(q_i, p_i) / \tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^B \exp(\text{sim}(q_i, p_j) / \tau)}$$
  • Temperature \(\tau = 0.05\): Fixed sharpening factor pushing representations to compact angular clusters.
  • Optimization Protocol:
    • AdamW (weight decay \(0.01\)) + Linear Warmup (10%) + Cosine Annealing decay.
    • Batch size \(B=16\), fp32 on CPU / fp16 on T4 GPU.

A1 Overfitting: Train loss plunged to 0.017 while val loss stalled at 0.86. From-scratch models cannot learn general semantic manifolds from 1.1k pairs.

A2 vs A3: A2's frozen backbone completely prevents overfitting; A3 fine-tuning achieves smooth convergence with warmup.

Why Did PrahokBART (A4) Struggle on T-Q?

  • 1. Pre-training Task Mismatch: PrahokBART was pre-trained for sequence-to-sequence text denoising (reconstruction), not sentence-level contrastive embedding.
  • 2. Capacity & Cross-Lingual Transfer: 35.8M parameters / 512-dim vs XLM-R's 278M parameters / 768-dim with massive multi-domain cross-lingual knowledge.
  • 3. The T-T Proof: On formal statutory titles (T-T), PrahokBART hits a strong 80.4% Hit@5 (beating A1 BiLSTM at 75.7% and zero-shot E5 at 72.3%), confirming its Khmer vocabulary quality, but lacks conversational semantic abstraction.

Three Core Project Conclusions
