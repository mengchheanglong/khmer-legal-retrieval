5. Training Mechanics & Objective ⏱ 4:05 – 4:50 (45s)

Contrastive Metric Learning: InfoNCE in Action

Pulling matching query-article pairs together while repelling in-batch negatives

In-Batch Negative Similarity Matrix

In a batch of B = 32 pairs, for query qi, passage pi is positive. The remaining 31 passages serve as negatives:

Diagonal = Positives · Off-Diagonal = 31 Negatives

Symmetric InfoNCE Loss (τ = 0.05)

Maximizes mutual information between query and passage representations:

L = - 1/(2B) * [ log(pos / sum_j exp(sim_ij/τ)) + log(pos / sum_j exp(sim_ji/τ)) ]

Why τ = 0.05? Sharp scaling creates strong gradient penalization on hard negative false positives.

Optimization Protocol (§5.B)
  • Optimizer: AdamW + 10% Linear Warmup + Cosine Decay.
  • Checkpointing: best.pt and last.pt with full RNG state and --resume.
  • Early Stopping: Monitored on Validation MRR@10 (patience = 5).

A2. Loss Function, Temperature & Evaluation Metrics

Mathematical formulations for InfoNCE contrastive loss, Recall@k, and MRR@10

InfoNCE & Temperature Dynamics

InfoNCE optimizes a lower bound on mutual information I(Q; P):

p(pi | qi) = exp(sim(qi, pi) / τ) ÷ ∑j=1B exp(sim(qi, pj) / τ)

When τ = 0.05, the similarity distribution becomes sharp, heavily penalizing hard negative false positives instead of diffusing gradients.

Evaluation Metrics Formulation
Recall@k: Fraction of ground-truth articles retrieved in top-k:
Recall@k = (1 / N) × ∑i=1N (| Rk(qi) ∩ G(qi) |) / (| G(qi) |)
MRR@10 (Mean Reciprocal Rank): Inverse rank of first relevant article:
MRR@10 = (1 / N) × ∑i=1N (1 / ranki)   [if ranki ≤ 10 else 0]
